THE ATTACK SURFACE GAP

Why v1 Oracles Fail Against Market Attacks

Traditional oracles only secure the communication layer. When the underlying market itself is distorted, honest reporting becomes the vector of exploit.

ORACLE LAYER (v1 SCOPE)

"Dishonest messenger"

Lying or late oracle

An attacker compromises a reporting node, forges price data, or exploits network congestion to delay feeds. The oracle reports a price that does not reflect real trading.

STOPPED BY v1:
  • Cryptographic EIP-191 signatures from whitelisted attesters
  • Multi-source quorum consensus requirement (N ≥ 3)
  • Hard staleness windows (≤ 60 seconds) & divergence checks
MARKET LAYER (v2 SOLUTION)

"Dishonest market"

Real price moved by capital

The attacker actually pushes the spot price on illiquid secondary orderbooks. Every honest source reports this manipulated number correctly and truthfully.

STOPPED BY v2:
  • Cost awareness: Calculates capital needed to move each feed
  • Price clamping: Clamps effective borrow price to TWAP if attack is cheap
  • Epoch cap: Enforces hard $50,000 borrow growth cap per epoch to strictly bound loss

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How Origin ASO v2 Works

An end-to-end multi-layer pipeline converting raw source observations into manipulation-cost-aware effective prices and bounded-loss borrowing parameters.

Figure 1: High-level dataflow connecting Source Ingestion, Quantitative Risk Engine, Sentinel Registry, and Downstream ToyLendingMarket.
01

Source Validation

Continuously polls 4 independent custodian, orderbook, and interbank feeds (Ondo, Coinbase, Kraken, Fed H.15). Enforces quorum (≥ 3 online sources), max age (≤ 60s), and cryptographic EIP-191 signatures from staked attesters.

02

Risk Engine

Calculates the liquidity-weighted median requiring >50% total capital depth to shift, tracks 5-period TWAP, and ranks sources by capital manipulation cost. Evaluates whether attack cost is cheaper than extractable borrowing value.

03

Sentinel Registry

An on-chain 6-state circuit breaker (FRESH, WATCH, STALE, DISPUTED, PROTECTIVE, RECOVERING). Reacts to risk signals by dynamically adjusting protocol debt ceilings between 100% and 0%, requiring a 3-consecutive-check streak to recover.

04

Lending Risk Controller

The credit facility (ToyLendingMarket) that consumes effective oracle pricing. Enforces collateral limits, verifies Sentinel debt ceilings, and gates every borrow transaction through a hard epoch loss budget.

THE THREE SYSTEM OUTPUTS

What the Protocol Produces for Downstream DeFi

1. Effective Price

Clamps to min(spot, TWAP, historical band) if the cost gate determines manipulation is cheap. Prevents phantom borrow expansion.

2. Epoch Borrow Growth Cap

Strictly caps cumulative borrowing expansion to $50,000 USD per epoch, guaranteeing bounded worst-case damage even if an attack slips past detection.

3. Sentinel Debt Ceiling

Dynamic protocol-wide ceiling adjusting from $500,000 (100% in FRESH) to $150,000 (30% in PROTECTIVE) and $0 (in DISPUTED). Repayment is always ungated.

ECONOMIC & CRYPTOGRAPHIC INVARIANTS

Three Guarantees

Layered defense ensuring that neither low-effort fraud nor sovereign-grade capital attacks can compromise protocol solvency.

01

Freshness stops the lazy attacker.

Attestation timestamps, cryptographic signatures, and strict 60-second staleness limits prevent delayed, replayed, or uncoordinated oracle reports from ever reaching the lending market.

02

Cost awareness stops the funded attacker.

By modeling orderbook depth and quadratic slippage, the Risk Engine requires that manipulating the price costs at least 1.2x the borrow it unlocks. Cheap moves are clamped to TWAP.

03

The epoch cap bounds the loss even if detection fails.

If a zero-day vector or massive whale squeeze successfully fools detection, the hard $50,000 epoch borrow growth budget strictly caps protocol loss to an acceptable, bounded balance sheet expense.

SENTINEL CIRCUIT BREAKER

The 6 Sentinel States

Autonomous state machine adapting debt ceilings to real-time risk conditions. Repayments are guaranteed to succeed in every state.

STATE CEILING MEANING & BEHAVIOR TRIGGERING CHECK
FRESH 100% ($500k) Normal healthy operation. Full borrowing capacity enabled. All feeds online, fresh, aligned, and cost-verified.
WATCH 80% ($400k) Cautionary threshold. Slight price velocity or mild TWAP drift. Velocity or TWAP drift exceeds soft threshold.
STALE 50% ($250k) Freshness degraded. One source offline or attestation age > 60s. Source server offline or attestation window timeout.
DISPUTED 0% ($0 Frozen) Critical breach. All borrowing halted; emergency freeze active. Vault share integrity jump or governance intervention.
PROTECTIVE 30% ($150k) Active attack clamped. Effective price forced to TWAP; debt capped. Manipulation cost gate detects cheap manipulation.
RECOVERING 25% → 75% Provisional recovery. Ceiling restores step-by-step across 3 cycles. Sources restored; requires 3 consecutive healthy checks.

CRITICAL DEFI INVARIANT: repay() IS UNGATED IN EVERY STATE

Regardless of whether Sentinel is FRESH, STALE, PROTECTIVE, or DISPUTED, borrowers can always repay debt and redeem collateral. Freezing repayments creates artificial liquidations; Origin guarantees open repayments unconditionally.

TRANSPARENCY & ASSUMPTIONS

Honest Limits

We believe in honest threat modeling. No security model is magic; understanding boundaries is how resilient protocols are built.

1. Cost figure is an estimate

Attack cost is calculated using on-chain liquidity weights, published orderbook depth, and quadratic slippage approximations. While highly predictive, live orderbook liquidity fluctuates with broader market volatility.

2. Loss bounding vs total prevention

We guarantee bounded loss rather than promising zero manipulation. A well-funded attacker willing to spend millions to move high-liquidity sources can still move price; the epoch cap bounds protocol damage to a survivable $50k budget.

3. Demo sources are mock servers

For deterministic simulation, local demonstrations connect to 4 realistic mock source servers (Ondo, Coinbase, Kraken, Fed H.15) on port 4000. In production, these ingest live institutional APIs and chainlink feeds.

LAYER 1 • BASIC CONSENSUS & FRESHNESS

Cryptographic Freshness & Quorum Check

Every attestation requires multi-source signatures with verifiable timestamp delta ≤ 60 seconds. If sources stop reporting or quorum drops below 3/4, Sentinel immediately halts borrowing.

Max Attestation Age: 60 seconds
Signature Format: EIP-712 / EIP-191
Quorum Requirement: ≥ 3 of 4 feeds
Attester Slashing: 1.0 ETH Bond burned on >50 bps divergence
LAYER 2 • QUANTITATIVE RISK ENGINE

Manipulation-Cost Calculation & Coalition Search

Computes capital needed to push the weighted median price by move m across all combinations of available sources.

C_cap,s(m) = R_s × (√(1+m) − 1)
C_cap,med(m) = min_{S, ∑w'_s ≥ 0.5} ∑_{s ∈ S} C_cap,s(m)
C_net(m) = ρ × C_cap,med(m) [ρ = 0.1412 measured in Part D]
A7.i Slow Ratchet Anchor: Measures move m against 24h slow anchor with max 1.00% drift rate per epoch, neutralizing drip/creep pumps.
A7.ii Fake Depth Defense: Uses MINIMUM depth seen over last 12 cycles in ring buffer, neutralizing flash liquidity injection.
LAYER 3 • DYNAMIC BOUNDED LOSS

Cost-Anchored Debt Ceiling & Epoch Growth Cap

Couples credit capacity strictly to verified attack cost. Under any market push, attacker loses more capital than maximum extra debt extracted.

debtCeiling = min(configuredCeiling, Γ)
epochGrowthCap = g × Γ [g = 20%]
